**🕒 15:03 👤 @trey.io (Trey Hunner)**
💬 Python Tip #297 (of 365):

Avoid making metaclasses if you can. 🧵

There's usually an easier tool for the job.

Metaclasses customize the behavior of CLASSES the same way classes customize the behavior of their instances.

#Python #DailyPythonTip

[Открыть пост](https://bsky.app/profile/trey.io/post/3mymxmxr7ol2s)

----
**🕒 15:03 👤 @trey.io (Trey Hunner)**
💬 Metaclasses are powerful, but they can also make for code that's overly complex and hard to understand.

Fortunately, many problems that COULD be solved with a metaclass often have a simpler solution.

[Открыть пост](https://bsky.app/profile/trey.io/post/3mymxmxtvni2f)

----
**🕒 15:03 👤 @trey.io (Trey Hunner)**
💬 You probably DON'T need a metaclass to:

• Customize instance creation: __new__
• React to subclassing: __init_subclass__
• Make a class subscriptable: __class_getitem__
• Modify a class once it's made: a class decorator

To customize your classes in some OTHER way, you may need a metaclass.
