**🕒 00:47 👤 @trey.io (Trey Hunner)**
💬 Python Tip #221 (of 365):

Implement appropriate dunder methods. 🧵

Dunder methods are a contract between your class & Python.

• __add__/__radd__ power +
• __eq__ powers ==
• __len__ powers len().

Don't shy away from implementing dunders when it might make sense to do so.

#Python #DailyPythonTip
